Privacy Policy

Extension itself do NOT collect any of your data.

This page exists solely because some stores require a privacy policy; it does not imply that our extensions collect data.

When you visit the extension's help site, service providers (such as Cloudflare, Tencent EdgeOne, etc.) may collect certain basic information, such as your IP address and browser type.

Extension may integrates some third-party services. When you actively use these services, the service providers may access your information. These services are not essential to the extension's core functionality and are beyond our control. You may review the privacy policies of these third-party services on your own, or simply choose not to use them.

The table below lists the third-party services integrated into our extensions:

Extension Function Third-party Services
Header Editor Options - Import and Export OneDrive / Google Drive / Yandex
Nebula Shift Tab Options - Backup OneDrive / Google Drive / Yandex
Nebula Shift Tab Options - General Settings - Icon Provider Google / DuckDuckGo / ToolB / icon.horse / Favicon.im / favicon.run
Nebula Shift Tab Options - Background Settings - Built-in Wallpaper Bing / Spotlight

隐私策略

扩展本身不会收集您的任何数据。
